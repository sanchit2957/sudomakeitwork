# Safety Tab Development Audit

## 22 August 2026

The Safety tab already connected verified shelters and Medical Operations resources to authenticated assistance requests, but it lacked a quick personal readiness plan, explicit location status, distance-first resource ordering, and current river-gauge presentation.

The enhanced mobile Safety view was visually checked in dark mode. It rendered the three-step device-local readiness plan, visible emergency escalation actions, a location-aware verified-support section, category selection, current medical capacity, request controls, concise flood safety cards, and a high-risk weather state. The current CWC-attributed gauge appeared as **Guwahati D.C. Court · 47.32 m · falling** with its source link. Interacting with **Higher ground** changed the checklist from **0/3** to **1/3 ready**, confirming the local readiness state responds as intended and does not send an operational notification.
